# Phoenix / Tempe transit, shuttle and ride facts used by Grocery Run

Checked October 2026. Fares, routes and offers change; when unsure, tell the student to check the official app or site.

## Priority order

1. Free: ASU intercampus shuttles, Tempe Orbit, walking.
2. Valley Metro light rail and buses (capped at $4 a day).
3. Rides: Uber, Lyft, Waymo, with any promo that applies.
Mixed trips (free or transit there, ride back) are often best for heavy loads.

## ASU intercampus shuttles (free)

- Free for riders: no ticket or pass needed. Carry an ASU ID card.
- Connect the four ASU campuses. Schedules and stops:
  https://cfo.asu.edu/shuttles
- Check the schedule for the day: service can differ on weekends and during breaks.

Source:
https://students.asu.edu/intercampus-shuttles-keep-sun-devils-move?page=0

## Tempe Orbit (free neighborhood shuttles)

- Free, no ID needed. Routes: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn.
- Run 7 days a week, about every 15 minutes (about every 30 on Sundays).
- Weekday "Short Trip" buses on Mars and Mercury during ASU semesters stop at the end of their zone; check the sign if going farther.

Source:
https://www.tempe.gov/government/transportation-and-sustainability/transportation/bus-light-rail-paratransit-streetcar/orbit

## Valley Metro (light rail and local buses)

- Fares are capped: local service costs no more than $4 per day, $20 per week, or $64 per month, however many trips you take.
- The cap only works when you pay with a Copper card or the Valley Metro app and tap/scan on every ride. A single cash ride is also possible, but it does not count toward the cap.
- Copper card: $4 issuance fee; load money and tap. The app wallet takes Apple Pay, Google Pay or a card. A rider uses one or the other, not both.
- RAPID express buses have a higher cap ($6.50 per day).
- Round trip on local transit for one person: at most $4 for the day.

Sources:
https://coppercourier.com/local/valley-metro-fare-explainer/
https://hoodline.com/2026/06/valley-metro-plots-fare-shakeup-phoenix-riders-set-to-sound-off/

## Waymo (driverless rides)

- Covers more than 350 square miles of metro Phoenix after an August 2026 expansion that added Gilbert and more of Chandler; earlier expansions added parts of Tempe and Scottsdale.
- Coverage is not everywhere. If the pickup or the store is outside the service area shown in the app, skip Waymo.

Sources:
https://www.azfamily.com/2026/08/13/waymo-expands-service-by-55-square-miles-adding-gilbert-chandler-neighborhoods/
https://www.abc15.com/news/local-news/waymo-expands-service-into-areas-of-scottsdale-tempe

## Ride discounts

- Uber One for Students: 4 weeks free, then $4.99 a month (half the regular price), for qualifying students; offer listed as valid through December 31, 2026. It's a subscription, so it only pays off for frequent riders. Check the current terms in the Uber app.
- Promo emails from the ride apps in the student's own inbox are the most reliable codes.
- Reddit codes are unverified. New-rider and referral codes only work for accounts that have never ridden with that app. Never suggest extra accounts.

Source:
https://www.dealnews.com/Uber-One-Student-Discount-Free-4-week-trial-50-off-free-shipping/21907287.html

## Practical rules for grocery trips

- Heavy loads (rice bags, oil, bulk Costco runs) are hard on a bus with transfers. Prefer a free shuttle or transit there and a ride back.
- Walking more than about 10 minutes in heat over 100°F is unpleasant and can be unsafe; prefer a ride or a cooler time.
- Some stores are far from free routes and light rail (for example Chandler). If a free route takes more than about 75 minutes, or transit more than about 90 minutes each way, say it is not practical.
